# DriveX App — Core Features (from the approved UI)

Every feature below is taken from the screens in Kamyar's design. Nothing new was added.

## 1. Welcome & Sign-in
- 3 welcome slides: "Welcome to DriveX", "Book, Manage, Save", "Ready to Hit the Road" (Skip / Next).
- Continue as **Guest** or **Log in / Sign up**.
- Sign in with a **one-time code** (email in the design; mobile number planned), or with Google / Meta.
- Forgot password / reset.

## 2. Home
- Personal greeting ("Good morning, Emma").
- **Offers banner** — partner deals for DriveX renters (e.g. 30% off at a restaurant).
- **Pending invoice card** with amount, date and a **Pay Now** button.
- **Recent bookings** with status: Paid / Unpaid / Waiting.
- **Offers for you** — partner discounts (e.g. 45% off, 22% off).

## 3. Bookings (my rentals)
- List of my bookings with status: **Ongoing / Overdue**, price per day, Details and Invoice buttons.
- **Booking details**:
  - Car details: model, type, gearbox, **plate number**, odometer at pickup, A/C, GPS.
  - Reservation info: number, start and end date/time, **time remaining**.
  - **Invoice**: number, issue/due date, total, VAT 5%, discount, grand total, payment status, payment method, **Pay Invoice**, **Download Invoice**.
  - **Insurance**: plan (e.g. Full Coverage — theft, accident, fire), valid / expired, **Change**.
  - **Traffic fines**: violation, amount, date, **Pay Fine Online**.
  - Vehicle condition at pickup (photos).
- **Extend rental**: choose new dates → pay → "Extension confirmed" or "Extension failed — try again / WhatsApp support".

## 4. New booking (Phase 2 — daily rental)
- Choose **pick-up location** and pick-up / return date & time.
- Browse cars ("Dubai's Finest Offers", "Monthly Specials"), **search, sort, filter**, car details, **Choose**.
- Options: insurance (Basic free / Full Coverage), mileage (Limited 120 km/day / Unlimited), pick-up at our location or **delivery to your door**, additional driver, child seat, extras.
- Booking summary: daily rate, refundable deposit, total.
- Payment: **Cash on delivery**, **Card on delivery**, or **Bank transfer** (bank details + upload proof).
- **Chat & book on WhatsApp** for people who prefer to talk first.

## 5. Support
- **Emergency hotline** — for real emergencies, **limited to 3 calls per user** (counter shown).
  Locked for customers without an active rental.
- **WhatsApp support** with working hours.
- Warning: unnecessary emergency calls may lead to legal action.
- **UAE emergency numbers**, one tap to call: Police 999, Ambulance 998, Fire 997, Roadside 901, DEWA 991, Tourist helpline 800 5111.

## 6. FAQ
- Search box + common questions (modify/cancel booking, required documents, deposit, extend rental, accident/breakdown, late fees, renting without a credit card…).
- Call us / Mail us.

## 7. Profile
- Edit profile: name, phone, country, email, photo.
- **Documents**: choose Tourist or Resident, then upload —
  Tourist: passport, foreign driving licence (front/back), visa, international driving licence.
  Resident: Emirates ID (front/back), UAE driving licence (front/back).
- Payments, Security (fingerprint login), Notifications, Change language, Support & legal, Log out.
